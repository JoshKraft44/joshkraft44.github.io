A comprehensive interface designed unofficially inspired by Calgary Transit to enhance user experience and accessibility.

Project Overview

The Bus Ticketing Kiosk was developed following Task-Centered System Design (TCSD), from initial user identification and requirements gathering, through iterative prototyping stages, to a fully functional vertical prototype.

Challenges & Iterations

Significant iterations were driven by heuristic evaluations, identifying issues like inconsistent button layouts and missing shortcut functionalities for experienced users. Adjustments ensured a more streamlined and intuitive user flow.

Final Design & Accomplishments

The final vertical prototype successfully demonstrated usability, accessibility enhancements, and comprehensive task coverage including ticket purchasing, student verification, and group transactions.
